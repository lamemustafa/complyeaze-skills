## Fork Naming

A fork must use a distinct skill `name` in its `SKILL.md` frontmatter and a distinct listing
name on skills.sh, agentskill.sh, any Claude plugin marketplace, or any other registry.

This matters more than it does for most projects: registries auto-index GitHub, and the
skill `name` is the install identity. A fork carrying our `name` is a genuine confusion
risk for someone running `npx skills add`.

A fork must not present itself as an official ComplyEaze skill, update channel, distribution
or support channel.
