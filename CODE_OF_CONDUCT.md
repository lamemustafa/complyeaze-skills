# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

Be direct, be accurate, be kind. Disagree with the claim, not the person.

This is a repository about tax compliance. People arrive stressed, near a deadline, and
often confused by a system that is genuinely confusing. Assume good faith and answer the
question that was actually asked.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- **Posting another person's tax data** — PAN, Aadhaar, GSTIN, TAN, taxpayer names,
  acknowledgement numbers, challan identifiers, bank accounts, tax amounts, ITR JSON, or
  unredacted screenshots. This applies to your own data too: a public issue is forever
- Presenting a guess as an observation, or a memory as a documented fact
- Removing a refusal boundary — filing automation, credential handling, forged filing
  identity — without a design issue and agreement
- Using this project or the ComplyEaze name to imply government endorsement or
  authorised-intermediary status

## Enforcement

Report to **contact@complyeaze.com**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may
edit, redact or remove content, and may block accounts.

Content containing real tax data will be redacted or removed **without waiting for consent**
— exposure is the harm, and delay makes it worse.
